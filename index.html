What Goes Wrong

The 6 Ways AI Transformations Fail

70% of AI initiatives underdeliver. Here's exactly why — and how to avoid each trap.

🌊
Boiling the Ocean
Trying to transform everything at once. Massive initiative, no focused wins, team burns out before value appears.
✓ Fix: Pick ONE workflow. Prove it. Then expand.
🗄️
Dirty Data Problem
Deploying AI on inconsistent, incomplete, or siloed data. "Garbage in, garbage out" is especially brutal for AI.
✓ Fix: Data audit BEFORE any AI project begins.
🚫
Change Resistance
Underestimating the human side. Tools get deployed but teams don't use them — or actively work around them.
✓ Fix: Involve your team in design, not just rollout.
🎯
No Clear Problem
Adopting AI because it's trendy, not because it solves a specific, measurable business problem with an ROI.
✓ Fix: Define the problem and success metric first.
🏝️
Pilot Island
A successful pilot that never scales. The AI solution sits in one team and never connects to the broader business.
✓ Fix: Design pilots with scaling criteria built in.
🔒
Vendor Lock-In Trap
Building entirely on one vendor's AI platform without portability. When costs change or products sunset, you're stuck.
✓ Fix: Own your data and prompts. Use open standards.

Coaching Question

Who on your team will be an early adopter? Who will resist most? Do you have a plan for both?

The answer isn't to force adoption — it's to design an experience that makes AI feel like an upgrade, not a threat.

The Leader's Change Playbook
  • Communicate the why before the what
    Share the business reason before the tool. People accept change when they understand the purpose.
  • Create psychological safety
    Make it safe to say "I don't understand this yet." Shame kills adoption faster than anything else.
  • Find and empower your champions
    Every team has 1–2 early adopters. Give them visibility and let them pull their peers forward.
  • Celebrate early wins loudly
    A single person saving 5 hours/week, shared in a team meeting, converts skeptics better than any training.
  • Measure and share progress
    What gets measured gets managed. Track adoption and time-saved, not just tool installs.

Responsible Deployment

AI Ethics & Governance

The businesses that win long-term build AI systems people can trust

🔒
Data Privacy
  • Never input PII into public AI tools
  • Review vendor data retention policies
  • Ensure GDPR/CCPA compliance
  • Employee data use requires consent
⚖️
Bias & Fairness
  • AI inherits biases in training data
  • Audit hiring AI for discrimination
  • Test outputs across demographic groups
  • Human review for high-stakes decisions
📋
Transparency
  • Tell customers when AI is used
  • Document AI decision-making processes
  • Maintain human override capability
  • Regular AI audit trail review
📜
Legal & IP
  • AI-generated content copyright is unsettled
  • Review AI outputs before publishing
  • Don't use AI to infringe on competitors
  • Check sector-specific AI regulations
Your AI Policy — Minimum Viable
  • Define what AI tools are approved
    Create a short approved tools list. Default is: ask before you use.
  • Set data input rules
    "Never input customer names, financial data, or trade secrets into public AI tools."
  • Require human review of AI outputs
    AI assists, humans decide. Define which decisions require mandatory human sign-off.
  • Create a disclosure standard
    When does your team tell clients they used AI? Set the standard before it's a problem.
  • Schedule quarterly AI reviews
    AI moves fast. What's best practice today may be wrong in 6 months. Build in review.
